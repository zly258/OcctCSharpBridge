ABI 5 is the only supported native ABI for Bridge 3.

Rules:
- No ABI4 export baseline is retained.
- No compatibility adapters or compatibility-only exports are retained.
- Public native declarations, native definitions, and managed LibraryImport declarations must match exactly.
- Managed OcctNative interop must use LibraryImport with Cdecl; DllImport is forbidden.
- New native entry points use semantic names; version/Ex suffixes are forbidden.
